Adrian Strategy 15m · 5m · 1m

Gold scalping framework — multi-timeframe alignment with strict discipline
Not a proprietary system. This is a rules-based synthesis of established multi-timeframe scalping logic. No verified public track record exists under the name "Adrian Strategy." Backtest on demo before risking capital.
15m

Bias

Clear market structure: higher highs / higher lows (bullish) or lower highs / lower lows (bearish). No clear structure = no trade.

5m

Zone

Price pulling back into a meaningful area: previous support-turned-resistance, VWAP, or a recent swing level. Do not chase price.

1m

Trigger

A price-action confirmation candle at that zone: rejection wick, engulfing candle, or break of a small 1m structure in the direction of the 15m bias.

🟢 When to BUY

  • 1 15m bullish: Price making higher highs & higher lows, or clearly above 50 EMA with structure intact.
  • 2 5m pullback: Wait for price to pull back into a support zone (prior breakout level, demand zone, or 20/50 EMA on 5m). Do not chase upward.
  • 3 1m trigger: At that 5m zone, look for a 1m bullish rejection candle (long lower wick) or bullish engulfing candle. Enter at the close of that 1m candle.
Stop Loss Below the low of the 1m trigger candle or below the 5m support zone
Take Profit Most recent 15m swing high, or fixed 1:2 RR
Management Move stop to breakeven at 1R

🔴 When to SELL

  • 1 15m bearish: Price making lower highs & lower lows, or clearly below a key moving average with structure intact.
  • 2 5m pullback: Wait for price to rally back into a resistance zone (prior support-turned-resistance, supply zone, or 20/50 EMA on 5m). Do not chase downward.
  • 3 1m trigger: At that 5m zone, look for a 1m bearish rejection candle (long upper wick) or bearish engulfing candle. Enter at the close of that 1m candle.
Stop Loss Above the high of the 1m trigger candle or above the 5m resistance zone
Take Profit Most recent 15m swing low, or fixed 1:2 RR
Management Move stop to breakeven at 1R

⚡ The "Adrian" Discipline Rules

🕒 Session filter

Only trade London (08:00–12:00 UTC) and New York (13:00–17:00 UTC). Avoid low-liquidity Asian session chop.

📰 No news trading

Stay out 5 minutes before and after major USD news (CPI, NFP, FOMC). Spreads widen and stops get hunted.

💰 Risk per trade

Never risk more than 0.5%–1% of your account per scalp. Gold moves fast — leverage can hurt.

🛑 Max trades per day

Cap at 3–5 trades. Two consecutive losses = stop for the session. Revenge trading kills scalpers.

📊 Range filter

If the 15m is ranging (no clear highs/lows), do nothing. The strategy requires directional bias.

🧪 Backtest first

Run at least 100 demo trades with your broker's spreads and execution speed before going live.

Honest caveat: Gold scalping on 1m/5m has a lower natural win rate due to noise. Research suggests 1m setups can have a ~43% win rate without higher-timeframe filtering — which is why the 15m bias is non-negotiable in this framework. Treat this as a framework, not a guarantee. No strategy works in every market condition.
